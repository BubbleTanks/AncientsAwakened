[h1] IMPORTANT NOTICE [/h1]
The disabling and forcing configs have been removed and moved to [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3747493112]Ancient Configs Plus[/url]! It's recommended to download this for more control over your ancients.

If you're running into softlocks in game, make sure that you're on the right version number (you may have to unsubscribe and resubscribe). If you are Latest/Main Branch, the version number should have "-LATEST" on it.

[h1] Welcome to Ancients Awakened! [/h1]
This is a mod that'll add a bunch of new Ancients from various sources. Currently this mod adds 4 new Ancients, but more will come in the future after we finish up some other projects we're working on. Please direct any bug reports and balance feedback to the forum in the STS Discord server, we are much more likely to see it there.
Contributors: CrypticAurora & SoyTheProton (Plus assistance from #sts2-modding)
